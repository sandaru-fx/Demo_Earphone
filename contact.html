Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions below.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship globally! International shipping usually takes 5-10 business days depending on your location. Customs fees may apply depending on your country's regulations.

What is your warranty policy?

All Aura Audio products come with a standard 2-year limited warranty covering any manufacturing defects. You can extend this to 3 years by registering your product on our website within 30 days of purchase.

How long does the battery last on the Aura Pro?

The Aura Pro features an industry-leading 40-hour battery life with ANC off, and up to 30 hours with ANC turned on. A quick 15-minute charge provides 5 hours of playback time.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day risk-free listening guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, you can return it within 30 days for a full refund. Items must be in original condition with all accessories included.
